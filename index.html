About LCG Boston

Since 2003, LCG Boston LLC has provided reliable residential construction management across Greater Boston and Eastern Massachusetts.

As a fully licensed and insured general contractor, we manage your project with hands-on supervision, strict local code compliance, and clear communication from start to finish.

Our focus is simple: high-standard craftsmanship and total peace of mind for every homeowner we serve.

Build With Local Experts

From Bare Lot to Final Walkthrough

One team manages every phase of your project, so nothing falls between trades.

  1. Site Evaluation

    We walk the property, measure every room, and review zoning before a single number goes on paper.

  2. Permits and Foundation

    We file the permits and schedule inspections with your town, so the work starts on approved ground.

  3. Structure and Framing

    Every wall, header and opening is checked against the drawings and the Massachusetts building code.

  4. Roofing and Siding

    Roofing, siding and trim systems chosen to hold up to New England snow, wind and salt air.

  5. Kitchens, Baths and Finishes

    Cabinetry, tile, custom glass and fixtures installed by trades we have worked with for years.

  6. Final Walkthrough

    We walk every room with you, close out the punch list, and hand back a home ready to live in.

Questions Homeowners Ask Us

Still unsure about something? Call us at (781) 732-1899.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. LCG Boston LLC is a fully licensed and insured general contractor in Massachusetts. We share our license and insurance certificates with every client before work begins.

Do you take care of permits and inspections?

Yes. We prepare and file permit applications, coordinate inspections with your town, and keep every phase compliant with the Massachusetts building code.

How is my estimate prepared?

After the site evaluation, we build your estimate line by line from your actual scope: materials, trades, permits and schedule. You get a written proposal you can review at your own pace.

Can you help with design and drawings?

Yes. We work with architects and designers to plan layouts, select finishes, and produce the drawings your town needs for permits.

Do you build ADUs and additions?

Yes. We build attached and detached ADUs, in-law suites, multi-room additions and second stories, following local zoning and building codes.

Which towns do you serve?

Greater Boston and Eastern Massachusetts, including towns in Middlesex, Essex, Norfolk and Suffolk counties. Not sure if you are in our area? Send us your ZIP code.
