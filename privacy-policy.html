Who we are

This website is operated by LCG Boston LLC (“LCG Boston,” “we,” “us”), a licensed and insured general contractor serving Greater Boston and Eastern Massachusetts. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through lcgbostonllc.com and when you contact us by form, phone, text message or email.

By using this website or sending us your details, you agree to the practices described here. Our Terms of Use also apply.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request an estimate or get in touch, you may share:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and the best way to reach you.
  • The ZIP code and address of the project.
  • The type of project, your preferred timeline and any details you choose to tell us about the space.

During a site evaluation or while we work on your home, we may also record measurements, photos of the property, drawings, material selections, access instructions and billing details. We use these only to plan, permit, build and invoice your project.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. These server logs help keep the site secure and working properly.

To block automated spam, the estimate form keeps a short list of the times it was sent from your browser, stored only on your own device. It is never sent to us and clears itself after one hour.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your request, schedule a site evaluation and prepare your estimate.
  • To plan, permit, manage and complete your project, and to follow up afterward.
  • To send scheduling updates, proposals, contracts and invoices.
  • To answer your questions and resolve any concerns.
  • To keep records required for billing, taxes, warranties, permits and our legal obligations.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To protect this website and our business from fraud, spam or misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Calls, texts and email

When you give us your phone number or email address, you agree that we may call, text or email you about your request and your project. These messages are related to the service you asked about.

We will not send you promotional text messages unless you have clearly agreed to receive them. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help, at any time.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only when needed to serve you and run our business, with:

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as our website host, our email and phone providers, the form service that delivers estimate requests to our inbox (Web3Forms), and the lead management system run by our website developer, Eu Sou TS, which keeps a copy of each request so we can follow up. They may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • Project partners who need it to deliver your project, such as architects, designers, subcontractors and suppliers.
  • Town and state offices when a permit, inspection or other filing for your project requires it.
  • Authorities or others when required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our clients, our team or the public.
  • A future owner of the business, if it is ever sold or transferred, who would be bound by this policy.

Cookies, analytics and ads

Our own website code does not set cookies. We use Google Tag Manager to load the tools that help us understand how visitors find and use the site and measure our advertising, such as Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising tools. These tools may set cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as the pages you visit, the ads you interact with and when an estimate request is sent.

When you send an estimate request, the email address and phone number you entered may be shared with Google Ads and Meta in a protected (hashed) form, only to measure which of our ads led to your request. They are not used in our website analytics reports.

Some parts of the site are delivered by other companies, which receive your IP address and may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps shows our service area. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts and public content networks (unpkg, jsDelivr and cdnjs) deliver the typefaces, icons and scripts that run the site.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, manage Google ads at Google Ad Settings, opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on, and adjust Meta ads in your Facebook ad preferences. Our site does not respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above. If you request an estimate but do not hire us, we delete your request within a reasonable time. If you become a client, we keep your contact, project and billing records during the project and afterward as needed for warranty, tax, permit and legal purposes.

How we protect it

We take reasonable administrative, technical and physical steps to protect your personal information, including a secure (HTTPS) connection on this website, password-protected accounts and limiting access to the people who need it, in line with Massachusetts data security regulations.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by Massachusetts law.

Your choices

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update it.
  • Ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it for legal, tax or warranty reasons.
  • Stop texts by replying STOP, and stop emails by asking us or using the unsubscribe link when one is included.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them as required.

Children

This website is meant for adults planning work on their homes. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Please check back occasionally.
