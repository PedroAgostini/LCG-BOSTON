Before You Remodel

Have a question that isn't here? Call us at (781) 732-1899.

Can you remodel my kitchen and bathroom in the same project?

Yes. Choose Kitchen & Bathroom in the form and our team will talk with you about planning and coordinating both spaces as one project.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. LCG Boston is a licensed and insured Massachusetts general contractor. We can provide documentation as part of the project process.

Do I need design plans before I contact you?

No. Some homeowners come with plans ready and others are starting from an idea. We can coordinate layouts, finish selections, and architect or designer support when the project requires it.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

We coordinate the required permits and inspections as part of the renovation scope.

How does the estimate work?

The estimate is free. Tell us about your project through the form, our team reviews the details and contacts you to discuss next steps, including a site evaluation when appropriate. Your proposal is built around your home's actual scope.

Which areas do you serve?

We work with homeowners throughout Greater Boston and Eastern Massachusetts. Share your property ZIP code so our team can confirm availability in your area.
